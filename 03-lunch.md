# Lunch

Ten midday meals that lean on a hot, heavy pan for character. Most are 20–25 minutes from start to plate, sturdy enough to pack as leftovers, and varied enough to anchor a week of lunches.

---

## 1. Cast Iron Tuna Melt

A canned-pantry staple turned lunch luxury by a covered cast iron pan.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 1 (5 oz) can tuna, drained
- 2 Tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 stalk celery, finely diced
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped onion
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 4 slices whole-grain sourdough
- 4 slices sharp cheddar
- 1 Tbsp butter, softened
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Mash tuna with yogurt, Dijon, celery, onion, lemon, salt, and pepper.
2. Lay 2 bread slices on a board. Top each with cheddar, half the tuna, more cheddar. Cap with bread.
3. Butter the outside of both sandwiches.
4. Heat skillet over medium-low. Add sandwiches **uncovered**; cook 3–4 min until deeply golden underneath.
5. Flip, **cover**, cook 2–3 min until cheese melts and the second side is golden.

> First side uncovered for crispness, second side covered to drive steam through and melt the cheese.

## 2. Mediterranean Sautéed Shrimp & Zucchini

A bright, lemony one-pan lunch with chickpeas for staying power.

**Time:** 22 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tsp each cumin, oregano, paprika
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat shrimp dry; toss with all the spices, salt, and pepper.
2. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium. Add onion and half the garlic; cook 3 min.
3. Add zucchini, chickpeas, salt, and pepper. Cook 5–7 min, tossing, until tender and lightly charred. Move to a plate.
4. Add remaining oil, shrimp, and garlic. Cook 2–3 min over medium-high, flipping once, until just pink and opaque — do not overcook.
5. Return vegetables. Add tomatoes and lemon juice; toss 1 min until tomatoes blister.

> High retained heat sears shrimp without overcooking, and the wide pan blisters chickpeas and zucchini at the same time.

## 3. Crispy Mushroom & Spinach Quesadillas

The shatter-crisp tortilla a thin pan can never deliver.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ red onion, chopped
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 4 oz baby spinach
- ½ lime, juiced
- 4 whole-wheat tortillas
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Salt

**Method**
1. Heat oil in skillet over medium. Cook onion with a pinch of salt 2 min.
2. Add mushrooms; cook 6 min until golden. Stir in spinach until wilted; finish with lime juice. Move filling to a bowl. Wipe pan.
3. On each tortilla, spread ¼ cup cheese over half. Top with mushroom mixture, avocado slices, and another ¼ cup cheese. Fold tortilla closed.
4. Return pan to medium. Cook two folded quesadillas at a time, 2–3 min per side, pressing with a spatula until tortilla is crisp and cheese melts.

> Trick: sprinkle a little extra cheese directly on the pan to fry into a frico edge.

## 4. 20-Minute Caprese Chicken

Hot pan, thin chicken, melted mozzarella, summer tomato.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb thin chicken cutlets (or breasts pounded thin)
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 thick slices fresh mozzarella
- 1 large tomato, sliced
- Fresh basil leaves
- 2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat chicken dry; season generously.
2. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
3. Sear chicken 2–3 min per side until deeply golden and 165°F.
4. Lay a slice of mozzarella on each cutlet, add 1 Tbsp water to the pan, and cover for 60 sec until cheese melts.
5. Off heat, top each with tomato slices and torn basil. Drizzle with balsamic.

> A roaring-hot, heavy pan delivers a mahogany crust on chicken in a few minutes flat — the crust is what makes thin cutlets feel restaurant-quality.

## 5. Mediterranean Baked Cod

Pan-seared, oven-finished, lemon-bright.

**Time:** 22 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1½ lb cod fillets, patted dry
- 4 Tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- ⅓ cup whole-wheat flour
- 1 tsp paprika, ¾ tsp cumin
- Fresh parsley
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Whisk lemon juice and 2 Tbsp oil in a shallow bowl; reserve. Mix flour with paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper.
3. Brush each fillet with the lemon mixture, then dredge in seasoned flour.
4. Heat remaining 2 Tbsp oil in skillet over medium-high. Sear fish 1–2 min per side, just for color.
5. Whisk garlic into the reserved lemon mixture; spoon over fish.
6. Bake 8–10 min until fish flakes. Shower with parsley.

> Quick stovetop sear on flour-coated fish, then carry that hot crust into the oven so cod cooks fast without drying out.

## 6. Korean-Style Ground Beef Rice Bowls

Crisp-edged ground beef in a glossy sweet-soy glaze.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1½ Tbsp honey
- ½ tsp grated fresh ginger (or ¼ tsp ground)
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Steamed broccoli, to serve

**Method**
1. Heat skillet over medium-high until smoking, ~3 min. Add beef in a single flat layer; **do not stir for 3 min** to let a deep crust form.
2. Break up and continue cooking 2–3 min until browned with crisp edges. Drain excess fat. Stir in garlic; cook 30 sec.
3. Whisk soy sauce, honey, ginger, and pepper flakes.
4. Pour sauce over the beef and simmer 1–2 min until glossy.
5. Spoon over rice. Top with green onions; serve with broccoli alongside.

> Press the beef flat and walk away. The undisturbed sear is what produces the crisp-edged crumbles this bowl is built around — stirring early steams them gray.

## 7. Tuscan White Bean & Mushroom Skillet

Different from the dinner version: this one leads with deeply browned mushrooms.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 2 (15 oz) cans white beans, drained
- 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- 4 cups baby spinach
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper
- Crusty bread, to serve

**Method**
1. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium-high. Brown mushrooms in a single layer 4–5 min; remove.
2. Add remaining oil and onion; cook 3 min. Add garlic; cook 30 sec.
3. Add tomatoes, beans, oregano, pepper flakes, salt, pepper. Simmer 8 min until thickened.
4. Stir in mushrooms and spinach; cook 1–2 min until spinach wilts.
5. Off heat, stir in half the Parmesan. Top with the rest. Serve with bread.

> Pre-browning mushrooms in the same pan that simmers the beans builds layered, almost stew-like depth in 25 minutes.

## 8. Skillet Smashburgers with Quick Slaw

Cast iron's signature lunch. The thinner you smash, the more crust you get.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb 93% lean ground beef, divided into 8 loose balls
- 4 whole-grain rolls (or thick sourdough), halved
- 4 slices sharp cheddar, halved
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cups shredded carrot
- 2 Tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 Tbsp Dijon
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Toss carrots and red onion with yogurt, Dijon, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Set aside.
2. Heat dry skillet over medium-high until smoking, ~5 min.
3. Place 4 beef balls in the pan; smash each flat with a spatula (parchment between helps). Salt and pepper.
4. Cook 2 min undisturbed until a deep crust forms. Flip; top each with cheese; cook 30 sec. Move to a plate.
5. Repeat with remaining 4 balls. Toast cut bread in skillet drippings 1 min.
6. Build: bun, two patties stacked, slaw, top bun.

> A screaming-hot dry pan plus a hard smash is the entire formula. Don't crowd — work in two batches even if it feels slow.

## 9. Chickpea Shawarma Bowls

Vegetarian, packed with spice, 15 minutes start to finish, packs cold.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and patted very dry
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1½ tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp cayenne
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 cups cooked rice (or quinoa)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ cup Greek yogurt + 1 minced garlic + squeeze of lemon (sauce)
- Fresh parsley, salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Whisk yogurt sauce; chill.
2. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high. Add chickpeas in a single layer; do not stir for 3 min.
3. Toss; cook 4 min more until blistered and crisp at the edges.
4. Off heat, add garlic, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, cayenne, salt; toss 30 sec in residual heat — that's enough to bloom them.
5. Squeeze lemon over.
6. Build bowls: rice, chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, yogurt sauce, parsley.

> Adding the spices off-heat — to almost-burnt chickpeas — is the trick. They bloom in the residual heat without going bitter.

## 10. Skillet Tortilla Pizza

Twelve-minute lunch from pantry tortillas. The dry skillet sets a crust no oven achieves on a tortilla.

**Time:** 12 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 2 whole-wheat tortillas
- 3 Tbsp tomato paste + 2 Tbsp water + 1 tsp olive oil + ½ tsp oregano + pinch salt (sauce)
- 4 oz fresh mozzarella, torn
- 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil
- Optional: thinly sliced cremini, bell pepper

**Method**
1. Heat dry skillet over medium 2 min.
2. Lay tortilla in pan; toast 60 sec until starting to crisp.
3. Spread half the sauce; scatter mozzarella, Parmesan, pepper flakes, optional veg.
4. Cover, cook 3–4 min until cheese melts and bottom is crisp and blistered.
5. Slide out. Repeat with second tortilla.
6. Top with torn basil, slice into wedges.

> The dry, hot pan + lid combo crisps the bottom while steaming the toppings — a ~12-minute riff on a Neapolitan crust.
