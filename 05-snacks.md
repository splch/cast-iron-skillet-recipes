# Snacks & Small Bites

Eight whole-food snacks built on a hot, heavy pan. Every one is on the table in 15 minutes or less and uses 4–6 ingredients from the shared pantry.

---

## 1. Crispy Skillet Chickpeas

Crunchy outside, creamy inside — better than oven-roasted because the iron crisps without drying them out.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained, rinsed, patted very dry
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- ¾ tsp paprika
- ¾ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- Black pepper

**Method**
1. Pat chickpeas thoroughly dry — moisture is the enemy of crispness.
2. Toss with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a 10–12 inch skillet over medium.
4. Add chickpeas in a single layer. Cover; cook 3 min undisturbed.
5. Stir gently, drizzle remaining 1 Tbsp oil, cover, and cook 9–11 more min, stirring every 3 min, until deeply golden and crackling.
6. Off heat, leave covered 1 min. Serve warm.

> Covering traps a touch of steam to keep interiors creamy while the heavy iron crisps the exteriors.

## 2. Stovetop Skillet Popcorn

The healthiest popcorn there is — whole grain, good oil, your salt.

**Time:** 8 min · **Serves:** 4 (~10 cups)

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ cup popcorn kernels
- 1 Tbsp melted butter (optional)
- Salt

**Method**
1. In a 10–12 inch skillet with a tight-fitting lid, combine oil and 2–3 kernels over medium heat. Cover.
2. When the test kernels pop, pull off heat, add remaining kernels, cover, shake to coat, rest 30 sec. (Evens the temperature so all kernels pop together.)
3. Return to medium. Once popping begins, crack the lid slightly to vent steam.
4. Cook, shaking, until popping slows to one pop every 2–3 sec. Pull off heat.
5. Tip into a bowl, drizzle with butter if using, and salt to taste.

> The deep, heavy floor distributes heat so popcorn doesn't scorch — thermal mass beating thin pots.

## 3. Skillet-Toasted Rosemary Almonds

Cocktail-party quality from pantry staples.

**Time:** 8 min · **Serves:** 4–6 (~2 cups)

**Ingredients**
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
- 2 cups raw almonds
- ½ tsp flaky salt
- ½ tsp lemon zest

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium. Add rosemary; sizzle 30 sec.
2. Add almonds; stir to coat. Toast 5–7 min, stirring every minute, until nuts smell deeply nutty and turn one shade darker.
3. Pour onto a plate. While warm, toss with salt and lemon zest. Cool 2 min — they crisp as they cool.

> Almonds go from "just there" to "burnt" fast in a thin pan; cast iron gives you a wider window.

## 4. Blistered Tomato & Garlic Toasts

A 12-minute riff on cast iron bruschetta.

**Time:** 12 min · **Serves:** 4 (8 toasts)

**Ingredients**
- 4 slices whole-grain bread, halved
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves: 1 halved, 1 minced
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan (optional)
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat skillet over medium-high 3 min, dry. Lay bread in pan; toast 1–2 min per side. Remove; rub one side of each with the halved garlic clove.
2. Add 2 Tbsp oil to the hot pan. Add tomatoes and ½ tsp salt. Cook, stirring every minute, 6–7 min until skins blister and a few burst.
3. Stir in minced garlic, oregano, and pepper; cook 30 sec. Off heat, mash tomatoes gently with the back of a spoon.
4. Spoon onto toasts. Drizzle with remaining oil, top with Parmesan, and serve.

> Cast iron blisters tomato skins faster than non-stick — that char-spotted skin is most of the flavor.

## 5. Bell Pepper Egg Bites

Naturally low-carb riff on egg-in-a-hole. The pepper ring is the cup.

**Time:** 12 min · **Serves:** 2 (4 bites)

**Ingredients**
- 1 large bell pepper, cut into 4 rings, seeds removed
- 4 large eggs
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp crumbled feta (optional)
- Pinch paprika
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 10–12 inch skillet over medium.
2. Lay pepper rings flat; cook 1–2 min per side until just softened.
3. Crack one egg into the center of each ring. Season with salt, pepper, paprika.
4. Reduce to medium-low; cover; cook 3–4 min until whites are fully set.
5. Sprinkle with feta during the last minute if using. Lift bites out with a spatula.

> Cast iron's residual heat finishes the eggs evenly under the lid — the pan becomes a tiny oven.

## 6. Two-Ingredient Yogurt Flatbreads

Soft, blistered flatbreads with no yeast. Great with hummus, or as scoops for the bruschetta above.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4 (4 flatbreads)

**Ingredients**
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (full-fat)
- 1½ cups whole-wheat flour, plus more to dust
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp olive oil

**Method**
1. Stir yogurt, flour, baking powder, salt, and oil with a wooden spoon until shaggy. Knead in the bowl 1 min until smooth. Rest 5 min.
2. Divide into 4 balls. On a floured surface, roll each into a thin 6-inch round.
3. Heat a dry 10–12 inch skillet over medium-high until very hot, ~3 min. Brush lightly with oil.
4. Cook one flatbread at a time, 1½–2 min per side, until puffed with golden brown spots.
5. Stack under a clean towel to keep soft.

> A screaming-hot dry pan is the trick: flatbreads steam internally and balloon. Cool pan = flat and tough.

## 7. Skillet Cinnamon Apples

Lightly sweet, butter-glossed, naturally sweetened by the fruit.

**Time:** 12 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 3 firm apples (Honeycrisp or Gala), cored and sliced ¼-inch
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1½ Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- Squeeze of lemon

**Method**
1. Melt butter in a 10-inch skillet over medium.
2. Add apples, maple syrup, cinnamon, and salt. Toss to coat.
3. Cook, stirring every 2 min, for 8–10 min until apples are tender and the syrup is glossy and lightly caramelized at the edges.
4. Off heat, finish with a small squeeze of lemon. Serve warm — alone, over Greek yogurt, or on top of oats.

> The pan's heat retention forms a real caramel-style glaze on the apple edges — a thin pan won't.

## 8. Pan-Fried Honey Bananas

Two-minute caramelization. Crisp-edged outside, custardy inside.

**Time:** 8 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 2 ripe but firm bananas, cut into ½-inch coins
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 2 Tbsp honey
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- Optional: chopped walnuts or Greek yogurt

**Method**
1. In an 8–10 inch skillet over medium, melt butter with honey, cinnamon, and salt; stir until bubbling, ~1 min.
2. Lay banana coins in a single layer. Cook undisturbed 2–3 min until bottoms are golden and crackling.
3. Flip each coin and cook 2 min more.
4. Slide onto a plate. Top with walnuts or yogurt. Serve immediately — they soften as they sit.

> The honey must be actively bubbling when bananas hit it — that's what locks in the crispy crust before the fruit collapses.
