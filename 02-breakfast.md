# Breakfast

Ten recipes that turn the morning skillet into the most flexible breakfast tool in the kitchen. Most finish in 20 minutes; a few weekend specials reward a slow morning.

---

## 1. Classic Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato-pepper sauce. The single most iconic cast iron breakfast — and it earns it.

**Time:** 30 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- Pinch red pepper flakes
- 1 (28 oz) can whole peeled tomatoes, hand-crushed
- 6 large eggs
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 10–12 inch skillet over medium. Cook onion and bell pepper 5 min until softened.
2. Stir in garlic, cumin, paprika, and red pepper flakes; cook 1 min.
3. Add tomatoes with juices. Simmer 10–12 min until thickened. Season.
4. Make 6 wells; crack one egg into each.
5. Cover and cook on low 5–7 min until whites set and yolks runny.
6. Scatter feta over top. Serve from the skillet with bread.

> Cast iron's even heat keeps the simmer steady; the pan goes straight to the table.

## 2. Spinach & Feta Frittata

Adapted from America's Test Kitchen. The Kitchn's perfect-frittata ratio: 10 eggs · 3 Tbsp dairy · 3 oz cheese · 2 cups vegetables.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4–6

**Ingredients**
- 10 large eggs
- 3 Tbsp milk
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 8 oz baby spinach
- 3 oz feta, crumbled
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat broiler. Whisk eggs, milk, oregano, ¾ tsp salt, and pepper.
2. Heat oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium. Cook onion 3 min; add garlic, cook 30 sec.
3. Add spinach in handfuls; cook until wilted and dry, ~3 min.
4. Pour in eggs. Stir gently until edges set, ~1 min. Scatter feta on top.
5. Slide under broiler 2–4 min until just set and lightly golden.
6. Rest 5 min; slice into wedges.

> Stovetop-to-broiler in one pan. Residual heat finishes the eggs gently — no rubbery texture.

## 3. Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash with Baked Eggs

Sturdy meal-prep hash that crisps without going mushy.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 large sweet potato, ½-inch dice (~4 cups)
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 cups baby spinach, chopped
- ½ tsp cumin
- ½ tsp paprika
- 4 large eggs
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Cook sweet potato 8 min, stirring occasionally.
2. Add onion and bell pepper. Stir in cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Cover and cook 5 min until potatoes are tender.
3. Stir in spinach until wilted, ~1 min.
4. Make 4 wells; crack one egg into each.
5. Cover and cook on low 4–5 min until whites set and yolks runny. Serve from skillet.

> Sustained dry heat from cast iron is non-negotiable for a hash that crisps instead of steams.

## 4. Strapatsada (Greek Tomato-Feta Scrambled Eggs)

Four humble ingredients, fifteen minutes, deeply memorable.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 5 large eggs
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Beat eggs with ½ tsp salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium. Cook tomatoes 5–7 min until softened and blistered.
3. Add garlic, cook 30 sec. Stir in tomato paste plus 2 Tbsp water; cook 2 min.
4. Lower heat. Pour in eggs. Stir gently until just set, ~2 min.
5. Off heat, scatter feta over top. Serve with toast.

> Turn the burner off before the eggs look done — the pan's heat finishes them soft.

## 5. Skillet-Baked Oatmeal with Bananas & Berries

Weekend bake that feeds a crowd and keeps in the fridge all week.

**Time:** 45 min (10 active) · **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp butter, melted (plus more for the pan)
- 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 large eggs
- 1½ cups milk
- ¼ cup maple syrup
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 2½ cups rolled oats
- 1 tsp baking powder, ¼ tsp salt
- 1½ cups mixed berries

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 375°F. Generously butter a 10-inch skillet.
2. Whisk bananas, eggs, milk, maple syrup, melted butter, cinnamon, and vanilla.
3. Stir in oats, baking powder, and salt. Fold in half the berries.
4. Pour into skillet. Scatter remaining berries on top.
5. Bake 30–35 min until set in the center and golden at the edges.
6. Cool 10 min; serve with Greek yogurt and a drizzle of maple syrup.

> Pre-warming the buttered pan in the oven gives crisp golden edges — the best part.

## 6. Whole Wheat Dutch Baby

The dramatic puff is pure cast iron physics. Master this once.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 3 Tbsp butter
- 3 large eggs (room temp)
- ⅔ cup milk (room temp)
- ¼ cup whole-wheat flour + ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- To serve: lemon, berries, maple syrup

**Method**
1. Place a 10-inch skillet in the oven. Heat to 425°F.
2. Blend eggs, milk, flours, maple syrup, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until smooth and frothy. Rest 5 min.
3. Pull out the hot skillet. Add butter; swirl until bubbling.
4. Pour batter directly into the hot pan. Return to oven immediately.
5. Bake 18–22 min, oven door closed, until dramatically puffed and deep golden.
6. Top with berries, a squeeze of lemon, and maple syrup. Serve immediately — it deflates as it cools.

> A smoking-hot pan is what causes the steam expansion that makes the puff. No other pan does it as well.

## 7. Egg-in-a-Hole with Avocado

The fastest legitimate breakfast in the book.

**Time:** 10 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 2 slices whole-grain bread
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 2 large eggs
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- ½ lemon
- Red pepper flakes, salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Cut a 2-inch hole from the center of each slice with a glass. Butter both sides of the bread and the rounds.
2. Heat skillet over medium 1 min. Add bread and cutout rounds.
3. Crack one egg into each hole. Season.
4. Cook 2–3 min until bread is golden and whites mostly set.
5. Flip carefully; cook 1–2 min more for runny yolks.
6. Top with avocado, a squeeze of lemon, and red pepper flakes.

> Even, dry heat browns the bread without burning the butter — better than non-stick here.

## 8. Banana Oat Pancakes

Three real ingredients (banana, eggs, oat flour), tender pancakes, no refined sugar.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 2 (~6 pancakes)

**Ingredients**
- 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 4 large eggs
- ⅔ cup oat flour (or ¾ cup rolled oats blended)
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- Pinch salt
- 1 Tbsp butter, for the pan
- To serve: Greek yogurt, berries, maple syrup

**Method**
1. Mash bananas. Whisk in eggs.
2. Stir in oat flour, cinnamon, and salt. Rest 2 min.
3. Heat skillet over medium-low. Add butter.
4. Scoop ¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook 2–3 min until edges set and bubbles form.
5. Flip; cook 1–2 min more.
6. Top with yogurt, berries, and maple syrup.

> A preheated pan delivers steady low-medium heat — pancakes cook through without scorching.

## 9. Skillet Huevos Rancheros

Protein, fiber, and heat in one pan.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp chili powder
- 4 corn tortillas
- 4 large eggs
- 1 avocado, sliced
- ½ cup crumbled feta or sharp cheddar

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium.
2. Sauté onion 4 min. Add garlic and spices; cook 30 sec.
3. Stir in beans, tomatoes, salt, and 2 Tbsp water. Simmer 5 min until thickened.
4. Tuck tortillas around the edges of the pan; brush with oil. Make 4 wells in the beans.
5. Crack one egg into each well. Sprinkle cheese over the beans.
6. Bake 8–12 min until whites set and yolks runny. Top with avocado.

> Stovetop-to-oven in one pan: beans stay hot while the eggs bake.

## 10. Kale, Tomato & White Bean Skillet Eggs

A 15-minute weekday workhorse that eats like dinner.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced thin
- Pinch red pepper flakes
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 (15 oz) can white beans, drained
- 4 cups chopped kale (or baby spinach)
- 4 large eggs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium. Cook garlic and red pepper flakes 1 min.
2. Add tomatoes; cook 2 min until they begin to burst.
3. Stir in beans and kale. Cook 2–3 min until kale wilts. Season.
4. Make 4 wells; crack one egg into each. Sprinkle Parmesan over.
5. Cover and cook on low 4–5 min until whites set and yolks runny.
6. Serve from the skillet with toast.

> Lid traps gentle steam to set whites perfectly without overcooking the yolks.
