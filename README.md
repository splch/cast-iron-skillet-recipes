## How to Use This Book

**Most weeknight cooks need three things:**

1. A **short shopping list** for the week — pick 1 breakfast + 2 lunches + 3 dinners and the pantry handles the rest.
2. A **fast skillet recipe** they can actually finish on a Tuesday — most recipes here are 20–30 minutes.
3. A **reason to keep cooking** — flavor that comes from technique, not from ten extra ingredients.

This book is built around all three.

## A Final Note on Technique

Almost everything in this book hinges on one habit: **preheat the pan, then leave the food alone.** Three to five minutes of preheating on medium. Don't move the meat once it lands. Don't shake the chickpeas every ten seconds. Cast iron rewards patience with a crust that thin pans cannot produce. That crust is the flavor you came for.
