# Dinner

Eleven one-pan dinners that earn the cast iron its keep. Sear, roast, braise, and finish — all in the same skillet. Every recipe is on the table in 40 minutes or less.

---

## 1. Mediterranean Skillet Chicken with Tomatoes & Feta

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 Tbsp dried oregano
- ½ cup chicken broth
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 4 small tomatoes, diced (~1½ cups)
- ½ cup crumbled feta
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat chicken dry. Cut three shallow slits per side. Rub with garlic, salt, pepper, and half the oregano.
2. Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Sear chicken 3 min per side until golden.
3. Pour in broth, lemon, and remaining oregano. Cover, reduce to medium, cook 5–6 min per side until 165°F.
4. Uncover; scatter onion and tomatoes around the chicken. Cover; cook 3 min until tomatoes soften.
5. Top with feta and serve.

> A hard sear builds fond; the same pan turns into a quick braiser for the brothy finish.

## 2. Crispy Chicken Thighs with Carrots (Cold-Pan Method)

**Time:** 30 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2½–3 lb bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (5–6)
- 14 oz carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 2 small shallots, peeled and quartered
- 1 Tbsp butter
- 1 tsp fresh thyme (optional)
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 425°F. Pat thighs very dry; season generously.
2. Place thighs skin-down in a **cold** 12-inch skillet — no oil. Set over medium-high. Cook undisturbed 8–10 min as fat renders and skin turns deep golden.
3. Lift thighs out. Pour off all but 1–2 Tbsp fat. Add carrots and shallots; season; toss. Nestle thighs back on top, skin up.
4. Roast 18–20 min until chicken is 165°F and carrots are tender.
5. Stir in butter and thyme; rest 5 min.

> Starting in a cold pan renders fat slowly, producing the crispiest skin you'll pull from a home oven — no added oil.

## 3. One-Pan Lemon Chicken Thighs with Spinach Orzo

**Time:** 35 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 shallot (or ½ onion), thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup dry orzo
- 3½ cups chicken broth
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 2 cups baby spinach
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Season thighs with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
2. Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Sear thighs skin-down 5 min until crisp; flip, sear 4 min. Move to a plate.
3. Pour off all but 1 Tbsp fat. Add shallot and garlic; cook 1 min. Stir in orzo and lemon zest; toast 1 min.
4. Add broth, scrape up fond, simmer. Nestle chicken back in, skin up.
5. Bake uncovered 15 min until orzo is tender and chicken hits 165°F.
6. Stir spinach and lemon juice into the orzo. Sprinkle with Parmesan.

> Stovetop sear straight to oven: orzo cooks risotto-style in chicken-flavored fond.

## 4. Crispy-Skin Salmon with Lemon-Butter Pan Sauce

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 4 (6 oz) salmon fillets, skin-on
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon
- 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat salmon bone-dry — that is the secret. Season both sides.
2. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high until shimmering and almost smoking.
3. Lay fillets skin-down. Press gently for 10 sec for full skin contact. Cook undisturbed 5 min — skin should release naturally.
4. Flip; cook 1–2 min more (125°F internal for medium-rare).
5. Off heat, add butter, garlic, lemon zest, and juice. Tilt pan and spoon foaming butter over fillets for 30 sec.
6. Plate skin-up; sprinkle with parsley.

> Heavy iron retains screaming heat the moment fish lands — the skin sets into a crisp crust without sticking.

## 5. Blackened Salmon

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 4 (6 oz) salmon fillets
- 2½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ¼–¾ tsp cayenne (to taste)
- ½ tsp salt, ¼ tsp pepper
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp butter
- Lemon wedges

**Method**
1. Combine the spices.
2. Pat salmon dry. Rub with 1 Tbsp oil, then press the spice mix into all sides.
3. Heat skillet over medium-high a full 3 min — it must be very hot. Add remaining oil.
4. Lay salmon flesh-down; sear 3 min — do not move it.
5. Flip skin-down; add butter; baste 2 min until salmon flakes (125–130°F).
6. Serve with lemon wedges.

> Only cast iron holds enough thermal mass to char the spice crust without dropping temperature when cold fish hits.

## 6. Cast Iron Steaks with Garlic-Herb Butter

**Time:** 40 min (incl. rest) · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 (1 lb) boneless strip or ribeye steaks, 1½ inches thick
- 2 Tbsp neutral oil
- 4 Tbsp butter, softened
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 Tbsp fresh parsley
- 1 tsp fresh thyme
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Salt steaks generously; rest 30 min at room temp. Mix butter with shallot, garlic, herbs, and ¼ tsp pepper.
2. Place skillet in oven; heat to 500°F. Pull pan, set on stove over medium-high. Pat steaks dry; season with pepper.
3. Add oil; once it just smokes, lay steaks down. Sear 2 min per side undisturbed.
4. Reduce to medium-low; flip every 2 min until 120–125°F (medium-rare), 7–9 min total.
5. Rest steaks on a board, dollop with herb butter, tent foil, 5–10 min. Slice against the grain.

> Pre-heating in the oven loads the iron with massive thermal energy — sear in seconds, even pink edge-to-edge inside.

## 7. Apple Cider Pork Chops

**Time:** 30 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 4 boneless pork loin chops, ~1 inch thick
- 4 Tbsp butter, divided
- 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 Tbsp honey
- 1–2 sweet apples, sliced
- ¾ cup apple cider
- 1 Tbsp fresh thyme (or sage)
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 450°F. Pat chops dry; season. Whisk Dijon and honey; brush over chops.
2. Melt 2 Tbsp butter in skillet over medium-high. Sear chops 2–3 min per side.
3. Add apples, cider, and thyme; bring to a simmer.
4. Slide skillet into oven 8–10 min until pork hits 145°F.
5. Move pork to a plate, tent foil. Return skillet to medium-high; reduce sauce 3 min. Off heat, swirl in remaining butter.
6. Spoon glossy pan sauce and apples over pork.

> Sears, braises, and reduces in three back-to-back movements — the deep fond from the sear is what makes the sauce sing.

## 8. Sizzling Chicken Fajitas

**Time:** 30 min (15-min marinade) · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1½ lb chicken breasts or thighs, sliced ¼-inch
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp chili powder (+ 1 tsp for veg)
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt, pepper
- Warm tortillas

**Method**
1. Toss chicken with 1 Tbsp oil, the spices, half the lime, salt, pepper. Rest 15 min.
2. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over high. Sear peppers and onions with 1 tsp chili powder 4–5 min until charred at edges. Move to a plate.
3. Add remaining oil; spread chicken in a single layer. Sear 2–3 min undisturbed, then toss until cooked through, 3 more min.
4. Return peppers and onions, toss 1 min, finish with remaining lime.
5. Serve sizzling with tortillas.

> The pan reaches the screaming-hot temps needed to char peppers and caramelize the marinade in seconds.

## 9. Ground Turkey Skillet (Mexican-Style)

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 2 medium zucchini, quartered and sliced
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 large tomato, diced
- ¾ cup canned black beans, rinsed
- ¾ cup corn (fresh or frozen)
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1¼ tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- Lime, cilantro, salt

**Method**
1. Heat oil over medium-high. Add turkey; season with cumin, chili powder, half the salt. Brown 5 min, breaking it up.
2. Push meat aside. Add onion and tomato paste to the open space; cook 1 min, then stir together.
3. Add garlic, beans, corn, tomato; stir. Pour in ¼ cup water.
4. Add zucchini and remaining salt. Cover; cook on low 5 min until zucchini is tender-crisp.
5. Finish with lime and cilantro. Serve over rice or in tortillas.

> Non-stick steams ground meat. Cast iron browns it. That deep Maillard crust is the entire flavor base.

## 10. Beef & Black Bean Taco Skillet (Brown Rice Built-In)

**Time:** 40 min · **Serves:** 6

**Ingredients**
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb 93% lean ground beef
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 bell peppers, chopped
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 cup long-grain brown rice
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, rinsed
- 1 (15 oz) jar chunky salsa
- 1½ cups water
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high. Brown beef, onion, and peppers 4–5 min, breaking up the meat.
2. Stir in chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper; cook 30 sec.
3. Add rice, beans, salsa, water. Bring to a boil.
4. Cover; reduce to low; simmer 30–35 min, stirring occasionally and adding water if it looks dry, until rice is tender.
5. Top with cheddar; broil 2 min until melted and bubbling.

> Even, retained heat keeps the brown rice simmering steadily — no scorched bottom — and the same pan goes under the broiler.

## 11. Tuscan White Bean & Kale Skillet

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 (15 oz) cans cannellini beans, drained
- 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 6 oz kale, stems removed and chopped (~4 cups)
- 1 Tbsp red wine vinegar
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oil over medium. Cook onion 4 min until soft. Add garlic, pepper flakes, oregano; cook 30 sec.
2. Add tomatoes; simmer 3 min to thicken slightly.
3. Stir in beans and ¼ cup water. Mash a quarter of the beans with the back of your spoon for creaminess.
4. Pile kale on top, splash with water, cover, and cook 4 min until wilted. Stir in.
5. Off heat, stir in vinegar. Top with Parmesan.

> Iron's even heat lets the beans pick up a little caramelization on the bottom — the difference between "pot of beans" and a real skillet supper.
