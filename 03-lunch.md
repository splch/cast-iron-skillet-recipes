# Lunch

Seven midday meals that lean on a hot, heavy pan for character. Most are 20–25 minutes from start to plate, sturdy enough to pack as leftovers, and varied enough to anchor a week of lunches.

---

## 1. Cast Iron Tuna Melt

A canned-pantry staple turned lunch luxury by a covered cast iron pan.

**Time:** 15 min · **Serves:** 2

**Ingredients**
- 1 (5 oz) can tuna, drained
- 2 Tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 stalk celery, finely diced
- 1 Tbsp finely chopped onion
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 4 slices whole-grain sourdough
- 4 slices sharp cheddar
- 1 Tbsp butter, softened
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Mash tuna with yogurt, Dijon, celery, onion, lemon, salt, and pepper.
2. Lay 2 bread slices on a board. Top each with cheddar, half the tuna, more cheddar. Cap with bread.
3. Butter the outside of both sandwiches.
4. Heat skillet over medium-low. Add sandwiches, cover, cook 3–4 min until golden underneath.
5. Flip, re-cover, cook 2–3 min until cheese melts and the second side is deeply golden.

> Even ambient heat plus a lid traps steam to melt the cheese before the bread scorches. Medium-low is the sweet spot.

## 2. Mediterranean Sautéed Shrimp & Zucchini

A bright, lemony one-pan lunch with chickpeas for staying power.

**Time:** 22 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
- 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1½ tsp each cumin, oregano, paprika
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat shrimp dry; toss with half the spices, salt, and pepper.
2. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium. Add onion and half the garlic; cook 3 min.
3. Add zucchini, chickpeas, and remaining spices. Cook 5–7 min, tossing, until tender and lightly charred. Move to a plate.
4. Add remaining oil, shrimp, and garlic. Cook 4 min over medium-high until pink.
5. Return vegetables. Add tomatoes and lemon juice; toss 1 min until tomatoes blister.

> High retained heat sears shrimp without overcooking, and the wide pan blisters chickpeas and zucchini at the same time.

## 3. Crispy Mushroom & Spinach Quesadillas

The shatter-crisp tortilla a thin pan can never deliver.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- ½ red onion, chopped
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 6 oz baby spinach
- ½ lime, juiced
- 4 whole-wheat tortillas
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Salt

**Method**
1. Heat oil in skillet over medium. Cook onion with a pinch of salt 2 min.
2. Add mushrooms; cook 6 min until golden. Stir in spinach in batches until wilted; finish with lime juice. Move filling to a bowl. Wipe pan.
3. On each tortilla, spread ¼ cup cheese over half. Top with mushroom mixture, avocado slices, and another ¼ cup cheese. Fold tortilla closed.
4. Return pan to medium. Cook quesadillas one at a time, 2–3 min per side, pressing with a spatula until tortilla is crisp and cheese melts.

> Trick: sprinkle a little extra cheese directly on the pan to fry into a frico edge.

## 4. 20-Minute Caprese Chicken

Hot pan, thin chicken, melted mozzarella, summer tomato.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb thin chicken cutlets (or breasts pounded thin)
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 thick slices fresh mozzarella
- 1 large tomato, sliced
- Fresh basil leaves
- 2 Tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Pat chicken dry; season generously.
2. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high until shimmering.
3. Sear chicken 3–4 min per side until deeply golden and 165°F.
4. In the last minute, lay a slice of mozzarella on each cutlet; cover briefly to melt.
5. Off heat, top each with tomato slices and torn basil. Drizzle with balsamic.

> A roaring-hot, heavy pan delivers a mahogany crust on chicken in a few minutes flat — the crust is what makes thin cutlets feel restaurant-quality.

## 5. Mediterranean Baked Cod

Pan-seared, oven-finished, lemon-bright.

**Time:** 22 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1½ lb cod fillets, patted dry
- 5 Tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- ⅓ cup whole-wheat flour
- 1 tsp paprika, ¾ tsp cumin
- Fresh parsley
- Salt, pepper

**Method**
1. Heat oven to 400°F.
2. Whisk lemon juice and 3 Tbsp oil in a shallow bowl. Mix flour with paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper in another.
3. Dip each fillet in lemon mixture, then dredge in seasoned flour. Reserve the lemon mixture.
4. Heat 2 Tbsp oil in skillet over medium-high. Sear fish 1–2 min per side, just for color.
5. Stir garlic into the reserved lemon mixture; spoon over fish.
6. Bake 8–10 min until fish flakes. Shower with parsley.

> Quick stovetop sear on flour-coated fish, then carry that hot crust into the oven so cod cooks fast without drying out.

## 6. Korean-Style Ground Beef Rice Bowls

Crisp-edged ground beef in a glossy sweet-soy glaze.

**Time:** 20 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 3 Tbsp honey
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¼ tsp ground ginger
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Steamed broccoli, to serve

**Method**
1. Heat skillet over medium. Add beef and garlic. Break up and cook 5–7 min until browned. Drain excess fat.
2. Whisk soy sauce, honey, pepper flakes, and ginger.
3. Pour sauce over the beef and simmer 2 min until glossy.
4. Spoon over rice. Top with green onions; serve with broccoli alongside.

> Sustained high heat browns the meat into bits with crisp edges (rather than steaming it gray) before the sauce hits — the whole point of this bowl.

## 7. Tuscan White Bean & Mushroom Skillet

Different from the dinner version: this one leads with deeply browned mushrooms.

**Time:** 25 min · **Serves:** 4

**Ingredients**
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
- 2 (15 oz) cans white beans, drained
- 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- 4 cups baby spinach
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- Salt, pepper
- Crusty bread, to serve

**Method**
1. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium-high. Brown mushrooms in a single layer 4–5 min; remove.
2. Add remaining oil and onion; cook 3 min. Add garlic; cook 30 sec.
3. Add tomatoes, beans, oregano, pepper flakes, salt, pepper. Simmer 8 min until thickened.
4. Stir in mushrooms and spinach; cook 1–2 min until spinach wilts.
5. Off heat, stir in half the Parmesan. Top with the rest. Serve with bread.

> Pre-browning mushrooms in the same pan that simmers the beans builds layered, almost stew-like depth in 25 minutes.
